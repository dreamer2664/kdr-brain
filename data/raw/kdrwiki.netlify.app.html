The Crown

The reigning house of Dutch Robloxia.

King

Founder and sovereign since October 2022. Born 19 Aug 1984, Amsterdam. CEO of Goudhof Enterprises. Known as Your Majesty, The Crown, Lord Nicholas, or Nick.

Born 1995, Utrecht. Co-sovereign of the kingdom, married to King Nicholas Goudhof V. Known as the Fashion Idol. Eloise Castle and the Eloise Express are named in her honour.

House of Goudhof

King Nicholas Goudhof V

The man who built a nation from reclaimed sea and soil.

NameNicholas Goudhof V
Age39

TitleKing of the Kingdom of Dutch Robloxia
ResidencyCoastburgh Castle, Hendrikdam

Nicholas was born in Amsterdam and raised by his mother, whose identity has never been confirmed, though it is widely believed she had ties to the Dutch Royal Family. His father was a hardworking businessman who saw most of his opportunity in companies across America, building on wealth that had passed down through several generations of the family and pushing his company further than its rivals.

It was Nicholas's grandfather who first dreamed of building an entire country from nothing, purely as a business venture. He secured international permission to do it, but his health gave out before construction could ever begin. That task fell to Nicholas's father, who began importing soil and draining seawater along the coast between Belgium and the Netherlands. He succeeded in raising the land itself, but died of lung cancer at a young age, before a single building rose from it.

Nicholas inherited the family fortune, the land included, and gave it a name: the Kingdom of Dutch Robloxia. He proclaimed himself king. What had started as his grandfather's profit scheme became something else under Nicholas. Rather than building the country for himself, he built it for the people in it, securing civil rights, ensuring decent housing, and forging alliances abroad to strengthen the young kingdom's place in the world.

Character Biographies

The Realm in Person

Biographies written by the citizens themselves.

THE GERMAN EMPIRE

His Imperial Highness Albert of Germany

Prince of the German Empire · Scion of House Hohenzollern

Born May 10, 1871. Prince of Prussia and Prince of the German Empire. Residence: Imperial Palace, Berlin. Unmarried.

Son of Franz Wilhelm II, Emperor of Germany and King of Prussia, Albert is raised under strict royal discipline and trained in strategy, statecraft, and the values of order and loyalty. He accompanies his father to state councils, royal ceremonies, and diplomatic meetings. His name carries expectations, not just to inherit the crown, but to secure Prussia's place in Europe.

THE VATICAN

Pope Paul VII

Matthew P. Sollannius

Born April 30, 1978. Holds all titles belonging to the Pope. Residence: Vaticano. Unmarried.

Grown in Slovakia, climbed the church ladder, and became Pope in 2021.

REPUBLIC OF IRAQ

Prime Minister Arthur Al-Husseini

Prime Minister of Iraq & Commander-in-Chief

Age 43, born in Baghdad. Married. Base of operations: District 1, Executive Palace, Baghdad.

A former Iraqi special forces infantry officer and veteran of the counter-ISIS campaigns in Anbar and Nineveh. He left the military and entered politics, campaigning on strong-man security, an anti-proxy policy, and economic independence, and won the election to become Prime Minister. To guard against militias inside the state, he privately funds loyal tribal forces in District 3. He is restoring the country and its sovereignty.
